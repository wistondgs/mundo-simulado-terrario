## Scientific limits

Rival counts are not quality. Current predictions distinguish subset rivals mainly from a null, not every rival pair. Generated ablations have low purity until capacity, compute, temporal dynamics, and representation-distribution effects are matched. Metrics are specifications, not implemented measurements. Every result is invalid for treatment attribution if its manipulation check fails.

Negative results distinguish null findings, learning failure, interaction failure, instability, no behavioral disruption, and manipulation failure. None is automatically a refutation. Human decisions remain unset and require rationale.
